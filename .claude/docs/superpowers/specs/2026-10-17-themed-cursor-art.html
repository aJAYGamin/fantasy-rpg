Themed cursor — final art (10 shapes × 20 colours)

Source of truth for the cursor art in 2026-10-17-themed-cursor-design.md. Each shape is an SVG symbol whose paints come from the colour's palette (CSS custom properties --s0…--s7 light→dark, --rim, --flat, --border, gradients --grad/--night, and the shared dark --edge). AK and AR read their own slot maps (--ak*, --ar*), with Void, Obsidian and Lunar keeping the original cut. Top tile: over a dark menu; bottom tile: over the bright plains. The hotspot of every shape is its tip at (1.5, 1.5) — (2.5, 2.5) for AB — in symbol units.
